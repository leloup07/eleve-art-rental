2. DATA COLLECTED AND PROCESSED

Eleve Art may process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification data: first name and surname(s), company name (if the Client is a company), tax identification number (NIF/CIF).

  • Contact data: postal address, email address, telephone number.

  • Financial and billing data: bank account (for direct debits or refund of deposits), billing details.

  • Data on the installation space: delivery and installation address, relevant features of the space.

  • Communications: emails, messages and any other communication exchanged in the context of the contractual relationship.

No special categories of data (health data, ethnic origin, beliefs, etc.) are collected or processed.

3. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES OF PROCESSING

Your personal data are processed for the following purposes:

  • Handling requests for information, for a proposal or for access to the private collection that the user submits through the forms on the Website or the chat assistant. Legal basis: consent of the data subject, given by ticking the acceptance box (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and the taking of pre-contractual steps at the request of the data subject (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

  • Management and performance of the art rental contract, including formalisation of the contract, invoicing, collection of payments, delivery, installation and return of the works. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

  • Maintenance of the commercial relationship and communications relating to the service: notices regarding renewals, expiry dates, incidents and operational communications. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legitimate interest of the Controller (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR).

  • Compliance with legal obligations: tax, accounting and any other obligations to which the Controller is subject. Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

  • Sending commercial communications about new additions to the collection, available works or other information of interest relating to the service, only if the data subject has given express consent. Legal basis: consent of the data subject (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The data subject may withdraw this consent at any time.

4. RETENTION PERIOD

Personal data shall be kept for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which they were collected, and in any event:

  • For the duration of the contractual relationship and until the works have been returned and the security deposit settled.

  • Once the contractual relationship has ended, for the periods laid down by law for compliance with tax and accounting obligations (generally, four years under the Ley General Tributaria (General Tax Act) (LGT), and six years under the Código de Comercio (Commercial Code)).

  • Data from information requests that do not result in a contract shall be kept for a maximum of twelve (12) months from the last communication, unless the data subject requests their erasure earlier.

  • Data processed on the basis of the data subject's consent (commercial communications) shall be kept until the data subject withdraws consent.

5. RECIPIENTS

Personal data shall not be disclosed to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • The Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT) and other public authorities, where required by law.

  • Financial institutions, for the management of collections and payments.

  • Transport and courier companies, to the extent necessary for the delivery and return of the works.

  • Technology service providers (web hosting, email, document management), acting as Processors under the corresponding confidentiality and data protection agreements.

Contact forms. The data entered in the proposal request and private collection access forms are sent, via an intermediate server hosted by Railway Corp. (USA), to the email delivery service Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA), which forwards them to the Controller's mailbox. The date and time of acceptance of this policy are recorded together with the request. If automatic sending fails, the form opens the user's own email program with the message ready to send.

Chat assistant. The Website includes a chat assistant. The messages the user writes in it are sent, via an intermediate server (Railway Corp., USA), to an external artificial intelligence provider (Groq, Inc., USA) in order to generate the reply. If the user voluntarily provides an email address in the assistant, the Controller receives that email address and the content of the conversation in order to send the requested proposal. Users are advised not to include in the chat any personal data other than that needed to describe the project.

Access to the private collection. The full catalogue of the collection is hosted on a restricted-access site, protected by the Cloudflare Access service (Cloudflare, Inc.). To gain access, the authorised user enters an email address and receives a one-time code. The service records the email address used and the date and time of each access, for the purpose of controlling access to the collection and ensuring its security. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the Controller and the pre-contractual relationship with the user.

International transfers. Railway, Resend and Groq are providers established in the United States acting as Processors. The transfers are based on the safeguards provided for in the GDPR: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider has self-certified under it or, failing that, the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission included in their data processing terms.

6. RIGHTS OF DATA SUBJECTS

The data subject may at any time exercise the following rights:

  • Access: to know which of their personal data are being processed.

  • Rectification: to request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

  • Erasure: to request the deletion of their data where, among other reasons, the data are no longer necessary for the purposes for which they were collected.

  • Objection: to object to the processing of their data in certain circumstances.

  • Restriction of processing: to request the suspension of processing in certain cases.

  • Portability: to receive their data in a structured, commonly used format.

  • Withdrawal of consent: where processing is based on consent, to withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.

These rights may be exercised by written request addressed to coleccion@eleve.art, stating the right the data subject wishes to exercise and enclosing a copy of their Spanish national identity card (DNI) or other identity document.

If the data subject considers that the processing of their data does not comply with the applicable regulations, they are entitled to lodge a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (Spanish Data Protection Agency) (AEPD), through its electronic office at https://www.aepd.es.

7. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

The Website https://eleve.art may use technical cookies necessary for its operation. To the extent that analytics or tracking cookies are used, the user shall be informed by means of the corresponding cookie banner and their prior consent shall be obtained in accordance with the applicable regulations.

The user may configure their browser to reject or delete cookies, although this may affect the proper functioning of some features of the Website.

8. SECURITY

The Controller has adopted reasonable technical and organisational measures to ensure the security of personal data and to prevent their alteration, loss, or unauthorised processing or access, taking into account the state of the art, the nature of the data stored and the risks to which they are exposed.

9. AMENDMENTS TO THE POLICY

The Controller reserves the right to amend this Data Protection Policy to adapt it to new legislation, case law or customary practice. Changes shall be notified to the Client reasonably in advance and published on the Website. You are advised to consult this Policy periodically.
