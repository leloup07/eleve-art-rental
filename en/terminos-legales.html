The purpose of these GTC is to set out the terms and conditions governing the temporary rental of original works of art from the Eleve collection to natural or legal persons (hereinafter, the "Client") for display in private and corporate spaces, hotels, hospitality establishments, interior design studios and other similar spaces.

Any request to rent a work, whether made through the contact form on the Website, by email or by any other means, implies that the Client has read and understood these GTC and accepts them fully and unconditionally.

3. DESCRIPTION OF THE SERVICE

The service consists of the temporary rental of original works of art (paintings, photographs, sculptures, works on paper and other disciplines) belonging to the Provider's private collection. The service includes:

  • The temporary grant of the use and enjoyment of the physical medium of the selected work or set of works, for viewing in the agreed space. The rental does not include any intellectual property right in the works (see clause 11).

  • Transport and delivery to the address or space indicated by the Client, within the service area (Barcelona and its metropolitan area; other locations subject to a specific quotation).

  • Installation and hanging of the works in the agreed space, where this has been contracted.

  • A rotation or replacement service for works during the term of the contract, on the agreed terms.

  • The possibility of purchasing the work during or at the end of the rental period, with the amount paid as rent being applied to the purchase price, on terms to be agreed individually.

The rental of works of art for film or video shoots, photo shoots, audiovisual productions, advertising, campaigns, exhibitions or events open to the public requires a quotation and specific conditions agreed in writing with the Provider and is always subject to the Client first obtaining authorisation from the holders of the intellectual property rights in the works concerned (clause 11). Unless such authorisations are evidenced, the Provider shall not deliver the works for those uses.

4. CONTRACTING PROCESS

The service is contracted by means of the following procedure:

  1. The Client selects the work or works of interest through the Website or by direct communication with the Provider.

  2. The Provider issues a detailed financial proposal describing the works, the monthly rental price, the delivery conditions and any other particulars.

  3. The Client confirms acceptance in writing (by email or other reliable means).

  4. The Provider issues the corresponding invoice or supporting document, stating the amount of the first rental period and, where applicable, the security deposit.

  5. The contract shall be deemed concluded upon payment of the first period and of the security deposit, and delivery of the works.

The Provider reserves the right to reject any contracting request without having to give reasons, in particular where there are reasonable doubts as to the purpose for which the works will be used or how they will be treated.

5. PRICES AND PAYMENT TERMS

Prices for the rental service are expressed in euros and do not include VAT, which shall be added to each invoice at the rate in force. Where the Client is a consumer, the financial proposal shall state the final price including VAT. The price of each work is set out in the financial proposal issued by the Provider.

Unless otherwise agreed, the payment terms are as follows:

  • The first rental period and the security deposit shall be paid when the contract is formalised, prior to delivery of the works.

  • Subsequent periods shall be paid in advance, within the first five (5) calendar days of each period.

  • The usual payment method is bank transfer to the account designated by the Provider. Other payment methods must be expressly agreed.

Non-payment of any instalment that has fallen due shall entitle the Provider to: (i) claim the accrued instalments plus the applicable late-payment interest; (ii) suspend the service; and/or (iii) terminate the contract, with the works to be returned immediately.

6. SECURITY DEPOSIT

When the contract is formalised, the Client shall pay a security deposit equivalent to one or two months' rent (as agreed in the financial proposal), intended to cover any damage, deterioration or breach.

The deposit shall be refunded in full within fifteen (15) business days from the return of the works and their receipt in satisfactory condition, provided that they are in the same condition in which they were delivered, save for reasonable wear resulting from their proper care. In the event of damage, the Provider may apply the deposit to the corresponding repair or compensation, without prejudice to claiming additional amounts if the damage exceeds the amount deposited.

7. TERM AND RENEWAL

The minimum rental period is three (3) calendar months, unless a shorter or longer period has been expressly agreed in the financial proposal.

Unless either party gives notice to the contrary at least fifteen (15) calendar days before expiry, the contract shall be renewed automatically for periods equal to the initial period.

The Client may request early termination of the contract by giving at least fifteen (15) calendar days' notice. Under no circumstances shall rental instalments already paid for the current period be refunded.

8. DELIVERY, INSTALLATION AND RETURN

The works shall be delivered to the address or space indicated by the Client on the agreed date. The Client must ensure access to the space and suitable conditions for the receipt and installation of the works.

On delivery, a delivery record shall be signed describing the condition of the works. That record shall have evidential value in respect of any claims for damage.

The return shall take place on the same terms. The works must be returned in their original packaging or equivalent, and in the same condition in which they were delivered. Transport costs for the return shall be borne by the Client, unless otherwise agreed.

The installation service includes hanging on a standard wall. The Client must give prior notice of any special features of the space (special walls, unusual heights, specific technical requirements). The Provider shall not be liable for damage to walls or surfaces arising from installation and removal where these have been carried out correctly.

9. CLIENT'S OBLIGATIONS REGARDING CARE OF THE WORKS

The Client undertakes to:

  • Keep the works in suitable conditions of temperature, humidity and light, avoiding direct exposure to sunlight, heat sources, high humidity or corrosive environments.

  • Not carry out any intervention on the works (cleaning, restoration, handling or modification) without the express written authorisation of the Provider.

  • Keep the works in the agreed space and not move them to other spaces without prior notice to, and authorisation from, the Provider.

  • Not assign, lend or sublet the works to third parties.

  • Notify the Provider immediately of any incident, accident, damage or deterioration affecting the works, as well as any circumstance that could put them at risk.

  • Allow the Provider access to the works for inspection, subject to reasonable prior notice.

10. DAMAGE, LOSS AND LIABILITY

The Client shall be liable for any damage, deterioration or loss suffered by the works during the rental period, from their delivery until their return, regardless of the cause. This liability includes damage caused by third parties, theft, fire, flood or other contingencies.

The value of the works for compensation purposes shall be the value stated in the delivery record or, failing that, the market price of the work at the time of the damage, as determined by an independent expert. The Provider reserves the right to choose between repair, restoration or compensation for the full value of the work.

The Client is expressly recommended to take out contents or third-party liability insurance covering the rented works for the term of the contract. The Provider may require evidence of such cover as a condition for the delivery of certain works.

11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Provider owns the physical medium of the works but does not hold the intellectual property rights in them, which belong to their authors, their heirs or the collective management organisations that represent them (in Spain, mainly VEGAP). Accordingly, the Provider does not grant and cannot authorise any intellectual property right in the works.

The sole purpose of the rental is the temporary grant of the use of the physical medium for viewing in the agreed space. It does not entail the grant of any right of reproduction, distribution, communication to the public, making available, public exhibition or transformation in respect of the works.

The Client may not photograph, film, digitise, reproduce, publish —including on websites, social media and promotional material—, disseminate or otherwise exploit the image of the rented works, unless the Client first obtains, on its own account, the authorisation of the rights holders or the use is covered by a statutory exception. Private copying on the terms provided by law is unaffected.

Film or video shoots, photo shoots and audiovisual or advertising productions in which the works appear are always subject to such authorisations being obtained in advance, the processing and cost of which shall be borne by the Client. The Provider may help to identify authors and rights holders, but does not guarantee that authorisation will be granted.

The Client shall be solely liable for any use of the image of the works made without the necessary authorisations and shall hold the Provider harmless against any third-party claim arising from such use.

12. PURCHASE OPTION

The Client may at any time express an interest in purchasing a rented work. The purchase price shall be that agreed between the parties at that time. By way of guidance, the Provider may, at its discretion, deduct part of the rental instalments paid from the final sale price.

Any sale shall be formalised by means of a separate contract and shall not be concluded until that contract has been signed and the agreed price has been paid in full. Ownership of the work shall not pass to the Client until that time.

13. GROUNDS FOR TERMINATION

The following are grounds for termination of the contract, without prejudice to any claims for damages that may apply:

  • Non-payment of two or more rental instalments.

  • Breach of the obligations regarding care and conservation.

  • Assignment or subletting of the works to third parties without authorisation.

  • Moving the works outside the agreed space without prior notice.

  • The opening of insolvency proceedings, the dissolution or the closure of the Client's business.

  • Any other material breach of these GTC.

In the event of termination, the Client shall be obliged to return the works immediately and in perfect condition, and shall bear all costs arising from their return.
